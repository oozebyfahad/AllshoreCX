Security & Compliance

Security-led delivery for client-controlled workflows.

Security requirements vary between industries and clients. AllshoreCX can configure operational controls around approved systems, access requirements and client-defined policies.

Operational controls can include

Controlled system access
Role-based permissions
Secure connectivity & VPN
Managed workstations
Restricted data access
Password policies
Confidentiality agreements
Access termination procedures

Important compliance wording

Do not publish “HIPAA Certified,” “ISO 27001 Certified,” or similar badges unless the relevant certification has been formally obtained and verified.

Use precise language such as “client-defined HIPAA workflows,” “GDPR-conscious operations,” and “security-led delivery” where appropriate.
